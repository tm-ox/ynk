title: About
description: YES NO KLUB IS A YOGYAKARTAN BASED DIY COLLECTIVE ASSISTING LOCAL & VISITING ARTISTS WITH PROJECTS IN INDONESIA & SOUTHEAST ASIA.
---

<div>

Yes No Klub (YNK) brings together artists from different backgrounds and approaches, unbound by genre or conventional performance formats, through gigs, curated festival stages, club events, residencies and collaborations. Everyone is welcome, whether you already know experimental music or are hearing it for the first time.

Established in 2010 as a monthly experimental whatever event at Jogja National Museum in Yogyakarta, Indonesia by Wok the Rock ([Yes No Wave](https://yesnowave.com), [Mes 56](http://mes56.com/)) & Timothy O'Donoghue ([Oxen Free](https://oxenfree.net), [Bus](https://www.busprojects.org.au/)), YNK now consists of a core group of contributors responsible for operations as required.

From 2018 to 2022 YNK co-managed [Nusasonic](https://www.goethe.de/prj/nus/en/), a project with the German cultural centre [Goethe-Institut](https://www.goethe.de/ins/id/en/), Germany's [CTM Festival](https://www.ctm-festival.de/) for Adventurous Music and Art, Singapore's [Playfreely / Blackkaji](https://theobservatory.com.sg/BlackKaji) and Manila's [WSK Festival](https://www.instagram.com/wsk.io/).

Through the project YNK organised a five-day, multi-venue festival in Yogyakarta in 2018 with participants from Southeast Asia and Europe, and a two-month residency in 2019 for [Nadah El Shazly](https://www.nadahelshazly.com) (Cairo) and [Jessica Ekomane](https://www.jessicaekomane.com/) (Berlin). YNK also took part in events across both regions.

</div>
<div>

YNK has previously acted as an official host organisation in Indonesia for [AsiaLink](https://asialink.unimelb.edu.au/) (Australia) and, in 2020, furthered its international network through a collaboration with HuruHara for the inclusion of four Indonesian artists in the [AsiaTopa Festival](https://www.asiatopa.com.au/) in Melbourne, Australia.

During Covid YNK shifted its focus to the curation of content for external events, most notably [ArtJog](https://artjog.id) and the facilitation of a stage at [Pestapora](https://www.pestapora.com) with artists from Indonesia, Malaysia, Thailand, the Philippines, Vietnam, Cambodia, Myanmar, Singapore, Japan and Uganda. YNK also participated virtually, on a number of occasions, in [MUTEK Montréal](https://montreal.mutek.org/), the Canadian festival of electronic music and digital creativity.

Post-covid YNK has returned to semi-regular programming with electronic event Yes No Disko, continued curation for both ArtJog and Pestapora and sporadic Yes No Klub gigs.

As a small, independent initiative, YNK relies on everyone involved, from organisers and venues to performers, audiences and supporters, each contributing in their own way to making events possible and sustainable.

</div>
